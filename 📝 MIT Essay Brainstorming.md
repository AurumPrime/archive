# 📝 MIT Essay Brainstorming

MIT looks for "cultural fit" and "creative problem solving." Here are some themes to develop:

## 1. The "Monk Mode" Narrative
*   **Idea:** Connecting the discipline of Calisthenics with the mental grind of Competitive Programming.
*   **Angle:** How physical failure (missing a muscle-up) taught resilience in debugging complex algorithms.
*   **MIT Fit:** Resilience, discipline, and the pursuit of excellence.

## 2. Casablanca to Cambridge
*   **Idea:** Growing up in Morocco and the transition to global competitions.
*   **Angle:** The challenge of accessing resources and how you built your own "Archive" to overcome it.
*   **MIT Fit:** Resourcefulness and contributing to a diverse community.

## 3. The Vision for Jarvis/EDITH
*   **Idea:** Why you are building these tools.
*   **Angle:** Not just for technical glory, but to democratize high-level engineering tools for others in developing nations.
*   **MIT Fit:** Collaborative spirit and using tech for good.

## 4. The "Why MIT?"
*   **Idea:** Why specifically MIT?
*   **Angle:** Mention specific labs (e.g., CSAIL) or professors whose work aligns with Jarvis/EDITH.
