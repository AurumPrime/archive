## 🟢 Problem B: [ The 67th 6-7 Integer Problem ]
- **Link:** [Problem B Link](https://codeforces.com/contest/2218/problem/B)
- **Solution:** [B.cpp](./B.cpp)
- **Technical Insight:** 
To get the highest sum after changing six numbers to negative, you keep the largest number positive and change all other six numbers to negative. The smart formula is two times the maximum number minus the total sum.

## 🟢 Problem C: [ The 67th Permutation Problem ]
- **Link:** [Problem C Link](https://codeforces.com/contest/2218/problem/C)
- **Solution:** [C.cpp](./C.cpp)
- **Technical Insight:** 
To maximize the sum of medians in groups of three, you place the largest available numbers in the middle slots of each block and the smallest numbers at the edges.
