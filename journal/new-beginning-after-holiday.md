# A Clear Direction

## A New Beginning

Tomorrow is my first day at school, and I am starting a new phase of my life.

This year will probably be difficult. 1BacSM will require a lot of time, serious effort, and consistency. I know that I will have to spend many hours studying, and I am not sure how much time will remain for competitive programming and my other extracurricular activities.

Honestly, this makes me a little afraid of the future. I do not know exactly how everything will fit together. Maybe my routine will change. Maybe I will need to reduce some activities or become much better at managing my time.

But I do not want to stop before I even begin.

## My Direction

I will try to continue improving in the areas that matter to me:

- Competitive programming.
- English and Italian.
- Gym and calisthenics.
- Touch typing.
- Chess.
- Meaningful extracurricular activities.

I will not expect every day to be perfect. My goal is to stay consistent, protect my priorities, and use the time I have wisely.

## Competitive Programming

I hope I can keep around two hours for competitive programming, but I will first understand the real pressure of the school year. If I have less time, I will focus on quality instead of forcing a schedule that does not work.

I will continue studying algorithms, solving problems, reviewing code, and building strong fundamentals.

## Extracurricular Activities

I hope I can keep enough time for chess, touch typing, languages, and physical training. I also want to develop a useful chess activity at my high school and contribute in a real way.

I do not know yet whether I will manage everything. I will discover it through experience and adjust my system when necessary.

## My Promise

I will not panic because the future is uncertain. I will start tomorrow, observe what happens, and improve my routine step by step.

The important thing is to keep moving forward without losing the direction I chose.

## Final Reflection

> I am excited about this new beginning, but I am also worried about the time and pressure ahead. I will do my best to protect my goals while facing the demands of 1BacSM.
